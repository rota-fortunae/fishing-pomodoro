## fishing-pomodoro

A study timer that lets you fish during breaks to customize your study time! 

## Planned features:

- combine fish for shiny version
- sell fish for coins, use coins to buy cosmetics
- fishing minigame is like FNF rythm game (no stardew fishing because im bad at it)
- can only change settings/cosmetics during breaks
- purchasable cosmetics:
    - more catchable fish, hats, outfits, bobbers, fishing rods, tank decor, water color, frame, timer font
    - custom music, hats, fish
    - charms that increase your chance of catching a kind of fish 
- settings:
    - timer settings (including long and short breaks)
    - audio (seperate music, sfx, and alarm)
    - window size fullscreen v.s. windowed, make windowed dragable
